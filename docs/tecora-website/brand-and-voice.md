# Brand and voice

## Name

**Tecora** — product name, always capitalized that way in UI and marketing.

Avoid: TECORA (all caps lockup), “Tecora AI”, “Tecora Chat”.

## Mark

- Symbol: lowercase Greek **τ** (tau)
- Assets: `docs/Greek_lc_tau.svg`, toolbar `assets/icon.svg`
- In product: τ appears in a dark circular chip / dock

### Mark rules for the website

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Use τ as the hero brand signal | Hide τ in a tiny favicon-only treatment |
| Keep the mark crisp, mono, geometric | Add neon glow / purple aura by default |
| Pair τ + wordmark “Tecora” in nav | Invent a second mascot |
| Let brand lead the first viewport | Let a generic headline overpower the brand |

**Brand test:** If you remove the nav, the first viewport should still feel like Tecora — τ + black/white presence should remain unmistakable.

## Visual personality

- Minimal, calm, technical honesty
- Black / white / gray — not playful SaaS gradients
- Sharp small radii in-product (`4px`); website can be slightly more expressive but stay mono
- Premium through restraint, not through glow and pills

## Voice

### Traits

| Trait | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Direct | Short sentences. Say the product truth. |
| Calm | No hype stack (“revolutionary”, “game-changing”). |
| Honest | Call out local estimates, platform limits, what’s not done. |
| Privacy-forward | Repeat local-first without fear-mongering. |
| Plain | Prefer “folders and search” over “synergistic knowledge graph”. |

### Tone samples

**Good**

- “Everything stays on your device — no account, no backend.”
- “Not an AI wrapper. A layer that makes living across chat apps less chaotic.”
- “Local estimate, not platform quota.”

**Bad**

- “Unleash the power of your AI memory with next-gen sync.”
- “The only ChatGPT alternative you’ll ever need.”
- “Track exact remaining messages across all AIs.” (not true today)

## Messaging pillars (in order)

1. Cross-platform organization  
2. Local-first privacy  
3. Search + resume  
4. Portable export  
5. Clean, minimal craft  

## Tagline candidates

Primary candidates (pick one for hero):

1. **Your AI chats. One local library.**
2. **Organize Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini — without leaving your device.**
3. **Folders, search, and export for the AI apps you already use.**
4. **The local OS for your AI chat history.**

Supporting line:

> Not an AI wrapper. No backend. Nothing leaves the device.

## Logo lockups for the site

1. **Nav:** τ mark + “Tecora” wordmark  
2. **Hero:** large τ (or product UI frame) + brand-forward headline  
3. **Footer:** small τ + wordmark + sparse links
