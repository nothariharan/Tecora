# Problem and audience

## The problem

People don’t use one AI. They bounce between Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

Each site keeps its own history. None of them give you a shared library. So everyday friction piles up:

- Important chats are hard to find again
- “New chat” titles mean nothing a week later
- Folders/projects (when they exist) don’t travel across tools
- Backing up conversations + artifacts is awkward
- Bulk cleanup on native UIs is slow and easy to mess up
- Privacy-conscious users don’t want a third-party cloud reading their chats

**Core tension:** the models got better; the *memory layer around them* stayed fragmented.

## What users feel (emotional framing for the landing page)

- “I know I solved this last month… somewhere.”
- “My sidebar is a graveyard of half-finished threads.”
- “I don’t want another SaaS account just to organize my chats.”
- “I want Claude for some things, ChatGPT for others — but one brain for both.”

## Who Tecora is for

### Primary

| Audience | Why they care |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy multi-model users | Live in 2–3 chat apps daily |
| Students / researchers | Need recall, export, and structure |
| Builders / hackathon teams | Want portable archives and fast cleanup |
| Privacy-sensitive users | Local-only storage is the pitch |

### Secondary

| Audience | Why they care |
| --- | --- |
| Power users who pin / tag mentally | Want real folders/tags |
| People switching machines | JSON archive import/export |
| Teams demoing AI workflows | Cleaner story than “scroll the sidebar” |

## Who it is not primarily for (yet)

- Users who only ever open one chat app and never look back
- People who want Tecora to *generate* answers (wrong product)
- Orgs that need admin SSO / cloud team sync (out of scope for v0.1)

## Jobs to be done

1. **Resume** — jump back into the right chat across platforms  
2. **Find** — search titles and captured text without opening three sites  
3. **Organize** — folders, tags, pins that Tecora owns locally  
4. **Backup** — leave with markdown / archive / ZIP assets  
5. **Clean** — bulk delete with a safer queue than frantic clicking  
6. **Control** — turn off message capture; wipe everything  

## Landing-page problem statement (usable copy)

> You use more than one AI. Your history doesn’t.
>
> Tecora gives Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini a shared local library — folders, search, export, and cleanup — without sending your chats anywhere.

## Competitive framing (honest)

Tecora doesn’t compete with Claude or ChatGPT. It competes with:

- Chaos in the native sidebars
- Manual copy-paste backups
- Spreadsheet / Notion “chat logs” people invent
- Cloud “AI memory” products that require another account

**Differentiation:** on-device, no backend, sits inside the sites you already trust.
